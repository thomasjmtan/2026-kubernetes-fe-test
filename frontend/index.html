02 / 哲学 · philosophy

Three principles.
Nothing more.

  1. 簡

    Kanso 簡素

    Remove until it breaks, then add one thing back. Every setting has to earn its place.

  2. 間

    Ma 間

    Space is a feature. Clear boundaries between services, between teams, between alerts.

  3. 静

    Seijaku 静寂

    Calm in the middle of a busy city. Systems that run quietly and speak up only when it matters.

03 / 路線 · the line

From commit to city scale
in four stops.

  1. K01

    Push 押

    Connect a repository. Kanso reads your container and infers the rest.

  2. K02

    Build 築

    Reproducible images, cached layers, signed artifacts. No pipeline YAML to tend.

  3. K03

    Deploy 展

    Rolling releases across regions, with one-keystroke rollback when you need it.

  4. K04

    Observe 観

    Logs, metrics and traces on a single calm page. Alerts that respect your evening.
